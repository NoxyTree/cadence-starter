Frequently asked questions

Who is this for?

Solo documentary, explainer, faceless-channel and media-automation builders who use several tools and need a dependable workflow around them.

Do I need to be a programmer?

You need basic comfort opening a terminal and editing Markdown, CSV or JSON. Connecting custom tools requires reviewing command arrays or scripts.

Does it include AI or media integrations?

No third-party integration is bundled. The first local stage proves the preview, execution and logging path; creative and rendering stages remain manual until you connect tools you trust.

Is anything uploaded?

No. Cadence has no account, telemetry, background service or network client. External tools you choose to connect have their own privacy terms.

What licence do I receive?

One operator may modify and use Cadence for unlimited personal and commercial media projects. Redistribution, resale, team sharing and offering the tool itself as a service are excluded.
